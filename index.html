More than a cup

A little care in every step.

NORTH END is built around a simple idea: great coffee starts long before it reaches your cup. The team sources specialty coffee beans from around the world and from Bangladesh's Hill Tracts, then brings them together through roasting and brewing.

Whether you are catching up with a friend, taking a break from a busy day or simply enjoying a coffee on your own, the café is a place to pause and recharge.
